ABOUT ME

Making data easier to understand.

I focus on turning raw data into useful insights, clear reports and practical recommendations.

I'm Joecliff A. Wilson, a Data Analyst with a background in Physics and an interest in turning data into meaningful insights.

My work focuses on data cleaning, analysis, visualization and reporting. I use Excel, Power Query, Power BI and SQL to move from raw data to clear findings.
